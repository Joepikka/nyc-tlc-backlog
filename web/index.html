Requests in TLC cohort

How the dashboard counts requests

Coverage
January 1 through March 31, 2025. The cohort includes TLC requests created since January 1, 2024, including cases already open on January 1, 2025.
Closing backlog
Requests created before the next local midnight with either no recorded closure or a closure at or after that midnight.
Recorded closures
Requests with a closure timestamp in the selected dates. A recorded closure can indicate that an agency saw or began acting on a request.
Age
Calendar days since creation. Reports count cases that are open on the final selected day and at least 30 days old.
Reconciliation
Opening balance plus new requests minus recorded closures equals closing backlog. The project checked each day and each borough-by-problem group against source records.
